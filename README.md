## Purpose and business questions
Provide managers with reliable descriptive evidence about recorded medical insurance charges. BQ1: compare smoking groups; BQ2: examine age/BMI relationships overall and by smoking status; BQ3: compare regional means/medians and within-smoking-group patterns. Results are associations, not causal effects, forecasts or individual premium recommendations. A predictive model is optional and deliberately omitted to focus on the assessed criteria.
